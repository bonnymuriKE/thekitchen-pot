## How to Choose a Small Microwave

### What Size Is a Small Microwave?

Capacity is the inside volume. It does not tell you how much counter the microwave takes, so use this table of typical outside sizes.

| Capacity | Typical width | Typical depth | Typical turntable | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.5 to 0.7 cu ft | About 17 to 18 inches | About 13 to 14 inches | About 10 inches | One person, reheating, dorms |
| 0.8 to 0.9 cu ft | About 18.5 to 19 inches | About 14 to 16.5 inches | 10.6 to 12 inches | One or two people, most apartments |
| 1.0 to 1.2 cu ft | About 20 to 21 inches | About 16 to 18 inches | 12 inches or more | Two to four people, big dishes |

Depth matters too. Standard counters are about 24 inches deep, so a deep microwave can crowd your cutting board. Our [countertop organization ideas for a small kitchen](/blog/countertop-organization-ideas-for-a-small-kitchen/) show how to plan around it.

### Measure the Turntable, Not Just the Box

Measure your largest dinner plate edge to edge. The turntable should be wider than the plate, or the plate will catch on the walls as it turns.

Many dinner plates are 10.5 to 11 inches across. That is more than a 10-inch turntable can handle. If your plates are big, choose a model with a 10.6-inch or 12-inch turntable, or use smaller plates. Our guide to [what ceramic dinnerware is](/blog/what-is-ceramic-dinnerware/) explains what your plates are made of.

Measure your tallest container too. A large mug or a deep storage tub can be taller than a compact cavity, which is often only 8 to 9 inches high inside. Flat, stackable [airtight food storage containers](/blog/best-airtight-food-storage-containers/) solve this and save fridge space as well.

### How Many Watts Does a Small Microwave Need?

Watts are cooking power, and most small microwaves run 700 to 1,000 watts. Package directions are often written for higher-power ovens. At 700 watts, plan to add time, stir halfway and let food stand a minute before eating. At 900 to 1,000 watts, most labels work as printed.

A [Nordic Ware microwave egg poacher](https://www.amazon.com/Nordic-Ware-2-cavity-microwave-egg-poacher/dp/B00004W4UR/?tag=kitchenpot-20) handles breakfast, and our guide on [how to use egg poachers](/blog/how-to-use-egg-poachers/) covers the basics.

Microwaves run for minutes, not hours, so they are a cheap way to heat a small amount of food. Our list of the [most energy-efficient small kitchen appliances](/blog/most-energy-efficient-small-kitchen-appliances/) compares them with ovens and kettles.

### Inverter vs Standard: Does It Matter in a Small Microwave?

A standard microwave only has one real power level, which is full power. When you pick 50 percent, it runs at full power half the time and switches off the rest. That on-and-off pattern is why defrosted meat can cook at the edges and stay frozen in the middle.

An inverter microwave lowers the power itself and keeps it steady. The result is more even defrosting and gentler reheating. In this list, the LG and Panasonic have inverters.

If you mostly heat coffee and leftovers, a standard model is fine. If you thaw meat often or cook from frozen, an inverter is worth having.

### Is a Microwave Air Fryer Combo Worth It?

A combo makes sense when it replaces something. If you have no oven, or you were about to buy an air fryer, one combo can do both jobs on one patch of counter.

If you already own a good air fryer, a plain microwave is smaller, cheaper and simpler. Compare the space first. A compact [air fryer under $100](/blog/best-air-fryers-under-100/) plus a 0.7 cubic foot microwave can take less room than one large combo. Our [small ovens and toaster ovens for small kitchens](/blog/best-small-ovens-and-toaster-ovens-for-baking-in-small-kitchens/) guide covers the third option.

Combos also collect grease on the walls and fan cover, so wipe them after crispy cooks, the same way you would [clean an air fryer basket](/blog/how-to-clean-an-air-fryer-basket/).

## Clearance, Venting and Where a Small Microwave Can Go

Countertop microwaves pull in cool air and push warm air out through vents, usually at the back, sides or top. Block those vents and the microwave runs hot, which shortens its life and can become a fire risk.

- **Never inside a closed cabinet.** A countertop model is not designed for it. Built-in setups need a model sold with a trim kit.
- **Leave the gaps the manual lists.** Each maker gives side, back and top clearance numbers. Follow those over any general rule.
- **Give combos extra room.** In air fry and convection modes, the outside gets hot. Keep them away from walls, curtains and low upper cabinets.
- **Keep the top clear.** Do not stack bread, paper towels or other appliances on top.
- **Plug straight into the wall.** Skip extension cords and power strips for a microwave.

An open shelf can work if it meets the clearance numbers. A sturdy rolling cart is another good spot in a [galley kitchen](/blog/galley-kitchen-ideas/) where the counter is narrow. Set it high enough to leave the vent gap clear.

A small microwave shines with batch cooking. Cook once, portion into containers, and reheat during the week. That rhythm cuts waste, as our guide on how to [cook for one without wasting food](/blog/cook-for-one-without-wasting-food/) explains. It also helps to know [how long leftovers last](/blog/how-long-do-leftovers-last/) so your reheated meals stay safe.
