Cold brew is coffee and cold water left alone for half a day. The hard part is not the brewing. It's getting the wet grounds out cleanly.

That is really what a cold brew maker does. It holds the grounds, then strains them out without a sieve, a towel and a sticky counter. The right one depends on how much you drink and how much [fridge space](/blog/organize-a-small-fridge/) you can give up.

## Cold Brew vs Iced Coffee: Which Article Do You Need?

Cold brew never touches hot water. Iced coffee is usually brewed hot and then chilled or poured over ice. The two taste different. Cold brew is softer and less sharp, while iced coffee keeps more of the bright, fruity notes.

## How Cold Brew Makers Work: Immersion, Slow Drip and Fast Machines

There are three ways to make cold brew at home. Six of the seven picks above use the first one.

### Immersion

Immersion means the grounds sit in water for the whole steep. Then a filter separates them. The OXO, Toddy, Takeya, Hario and KitchenAid all work this way. It is simple, forgiving and cheap, and it makes the classic smooth, heavy-bodied cold brew most people expect.

### Slow Drip (Japanese-Style Towers)

Slow drip towers look like lab glassware. Ice water sits in a top chamber and falls onto the coffee one drop at a time. It passes through the grounds and collects below over three to four hours or more.

The result is lighter and clearer than immersion cold brew. But the towers are tall, fragile and cost several hundred dollars, and the drip rate needs watching. That's why none made this list. They suit coffee hobbyists more than busy kitchens.

### Fast Machines

Fast machines shorten the wait. The Breville uses a timed cold steep with unheated water. Others use pumps or a vacuum to push water through the coffee faster.

Speed comes at a cost. Less time in the water means less body and a lighter taste. Fast cold brew is good coffee, but it is not quite the same as a long overnight steep.

## What Grind Size Is Best for Cold Brew?

Use a coarse grind. It should look like coarse sea salt or dry breadcrumbs, not sand.

Coarse grounds release flavor slowly and evenly, and they are big enough for mesh and felt filters to catch. A fine grind over-extracts, tastes muddy and clogs the filter.

If you buy ground coffee, a bag marked for French press or cold brew is the right size. If you grind at home, use the coarsest setting on a burr grinder. A blade grinder makes a mix of dust and chunks, which leads to a muddy cup.

## Cold Brew Ratio: Concentrate vs Ready to Drink

The ratio is coffee to water by weight. Weighing is more reliable than scoops, because coarse grounds take up a lot of space for their weight. A cheap kitchen scale settles it.

| Style | Coffee to water | Example batch | How to serve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strong concentrate | 1:4 | 100 g coffee, 400 g water | Mix 1 part with 2 to 3 parts water or milk |
| Everyday concentrate | 1:5 to 1:8 | 100 g coffee, 500 to 800 g water | Mix 1 part with 1 to 2 parts water or milk |
| Ready to drink | 1:10 to 1:15 | 70 g coffee, 1,000 g water | Pour over ice as is |

Both OXO models are set up for the strong end, about 1:4. The Toddy lands close to that too. The Hario is built for ready-to-drink brew. If a cup tastes too strong, add more water. If it tastes thin, use more coffee next time rather than steeping longer.

Concentrate also takes up less room in the fridge. A quart of concentrate can make two or three quarts of drinks. If you are unsure about measures, our guide to [how many ounces are in a quart](/blog/how-many-ounces-in-a-quart/) helps with scaling a batch.

## How Long Should Cold Brew Steep?

Plan on 12 to 24 hours. Cold water extracts flavor much more slowly than hot water, so it needs time.

- **In the fridge:** 16 to 24 hours. Slower, and a little cleaner tasting.
- **On the counter at room temperature:** 12 to 18 hours. Faster, with a bit more body.
- **Over 24 hours:** Many batches start to taste flat, woody or bitter.

Start in the middle, around 16 hours, and adjust by taste. Steeping in the fridge is also the safer habit in a warm kitchen. Once you strain it, the brew goes straight back in the fridge.

## How Long Does Cold Brew Last in the Fridge?

Strained concentrate keeps for up to about two weeks in a sealed container in the fridge. It tastes best in the first week, then slowly turns flat. Several makers, including OXO and KitchenAid, give that same two-week guide for their concentrate.

Diluted cold brew doesn't last as long. Aim to drink it within a few days. Once you add milk or cream, treat it like milk and finish it the same day. Our guide to [how long leftovers last](/blog/how-long-do-leftovers-last/) uses the same "cold, covered, labeled" rules.

Two habits help. Always strain out the grounds before storing, since grounds keep extracting and turn the coffee bitter. And keep it in glass or a sealed pitcher, away from strong foods. A good set of [airtight food storage containers](/blog/best-airtight-food-storage-containers/) works if your maker doesn't come with a storage carafe.

If you won't finish a batch, freeze some in an ice cube tray. Coffee cubes keep iced coffee from going weak as they melt. The same trick from our guide on [freezing single portions](/blog/how-to-freeze-single-portions/) applies here.

## Filters and Cleanup: What to Expect

The filter decides how clean your cold brew tastes and how much work it is afterward.

| Filter type | Found on | Taste | Cleanup |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stainless mesh | OXO models, KitchenAid | Fuller body, a little sediment | Rinse, top-rack dishwasher |
| Fine mesh basket | Takeya, Hario | Similar to stainless mesh | Tap out grounds, rinse |
| Felt pad | Toddy | Very clean and smooth | Rinse with water only, store damp and cold |
| Paper bag or filter | Toddy, add-on for others | Cleanest cup | Throw away with the grounds |

Wet coffee grounds are heavy and clumpy, so the easiest way out is a firm tap into the trash or compost. Don't wash grounds down the sink. They collect in the drain trap, a problem we cover in [organizing under the kitchen sink](/blog/organize-under-the-kitchen-sink/).

Rinse mesh filters right after use, before coffee oils dry on them. Once a month, soak metal and glass parts in warm water with a little baking soda to lift the brown film. If you put parts in the dishwasher, keep them on the top rack, and run a [clean dishwasher](/blog/how-to-clean-a-dishwasher/) so old residue doesn't redeposit.

## Picking Coffee Beans for Cold Brew
