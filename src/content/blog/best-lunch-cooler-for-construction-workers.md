## Hard vs Soft Lunch Coolers

Both work. The job decides which is better.

**Hard coolers** have rigid plastic walls with foam insulation. They hold cold longest, resist crushing and can double as seats. They are heavy and bulky.

**Soft coolers** use fabric shells with foam insulation. They are lighter and easier to carry and store. Good ones, like YETI's, hold cold well. Cheap ones may last only a few hours.

On a hot site with long shifts, choose hard. For commutes and shorter days, soft is more convenient.

## How to Keep Your Lunch Cold All Day

A good cooler is only part of the job. Pack it well.

1. **Pre-chill the cooler.** Leave it in a cool place overnight, or put an ice pack in it for 30 minutes before packing.
2. **Freeze ice packs overnight.** Use at least two.
3. **Layer the cold.** Put one ice pack under the food and one on top. Cold air sinks.
4. **Freeze a water bottle.** It acts as ice in the morning and a cold drink by afternoon.
5. **Pack it full.** Empty space warms faster. Fill gaps with drinks or a towel.
6. **Keep it shaded.** Direct sun heats even the best cooler. A truck cab with AC is ideal.
7. **Open it less.** Every opening lets cold air out.

## Food Safety on the Job Site

Heat is the enemy of lunch. Bacteria grow fastest between 40°F and 140°F, according to the USDA.

- **Keep perishables cold.** Meat, dairy, eggs and cut fruit need to stay below 40°F.
- **Know your leftovers.** Our guide on [how long chicken lasts in the fridge](/blog/how-long-does-chicken-last-in-the-fridge/) covers safe storage times.
- **Pack safer foods for hot days.** Hard cheese, whole fruit, nuts, jerky and peanut butter hold up better than mayo-based salads.
- **Use sealed containers.** They stop leaks and keep food separate. See our guide to the [best airtight food storage containers](/blog/best-airtight-food-storage-containers/).
- **When in doubt, throw it out.** If food sat warm for hours, do not risk it.

## Cleaning Your Lunch Cooler

A dirty cooler smells and can grow mold. A few minutes a day keeps it fresh.

- **Empty it every evening.** Remove food, wrappers and ice packs.
- **Wipe it out.** Use warm water and a little dish soap.
- **Deep clean weekly.** YETI suggests mild dish soap and water for its soft coolers.
- **Tackle smells.** A paste of baking soda and water removes odors.
- **Dry it open.** Leave the lid or zipper open overnight so it dries fully.
