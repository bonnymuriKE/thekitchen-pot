Can you bake in an air fryer? Yes. Cakes, cookies, muffins and even yeast bread all bake well in one, as long as you change the temperature and use a pan that fits.

That makes an air fryer a real option if your oven is tiny, broken or too big to heat for four cookies. Below is the conversion rule we follow, the pan sizes that fit each basket, and small-batch examples with real times.

**The short version:** Lower the recipe's oven temperature by about 25°F and start checking at about three quarters of the time. Use a pan that leaves half an inch of air around it: a 6-inch round fits most 4-quart baskets. Bake small batches, cover the top with foil if it browns too fast, and never preheat with loose parchment in the basket.

## Can You Bake in an Air Fryer?

You can. An air fryer is a small countertop convection oven. Convection means a fan blows hot air around the food. In an air fryer, that fan sits very close to the food in a tight space, so it bakes fast and needs a few recipe changes.

Here is what an air fryer bakes well:

- **Small cakes:** a 6-inch layer, a snack cake or a mug-size cake in a custard cup.
- **Cookies:** four to six at a time, from fresh or frozen dough.
- **Muffins and cupcakes:** in silicone cups or a small muffin pan.
- **Quick breads:** banana bread or cornbread in a mini loaf pan.
- **Yeast bread:** dinner rolls, a mini loaf, cinnamon rolls, flatbread and small pizzas.
- **Fruit desserts:** crisps, cobblers and baked apples in ramekins.

What it does not do well is anything big. A 9 x 13 sheet cake, a full loaf of sandwich bread or a tray of 24 cookies belongs in an oven. If you need more room, read our guide to the [best small ovens and toaster ovens for baking in small kitchens](/blog/best-small-ovens-and-toaster-ovens-for-baking-in-small-kitchens/).

## How an Air Fryer Bakes Differently From an Oven

Three things change when you move a recipe from the oven to the basket.

**The heat comes from above.** The heating coil sits right over the food, often only 2 to 3 inches away. Tops brown and set before the middle is done. This is the main reason air fryer cakes crack or dome.

**The fan blows hard.** Moving air cooks faster than still air at the same setting. It is the same reason we tell you to lower the heat when you [bake cookies in a convection oven](/blog/how-to-bake-cookies-in-a-convection-oven/). The fan can also lift light things, like empty parchment or a loose foil sheet.

**The space is small.** Smaller space means faster preheating and less wasted heat. It also means you bake in small batches and the pan must leave room for air to move.

So you bake a little cooler, check a little earlier and protect the top. The small space also helps your power bill, since a 4-quart basket heats far faster than a full oven. ## What Temperature Do You Bake at in an Air Fryer?

Our rule is simple: **lower the oven temperature by about 25°F, and start checking at about three quarters of the time.** That means checking 20 to 25 percent sooner than the recipe says.

If a recipe says 350°F for 30 minutes, set the air fryer to 325°F and check at about 22 minutes. If it is not done, add 2 to 3 minutes at a time until it is.

Then adjust for the pan. Dark pans brown faster. Glass and ceramic heat slower and may need a few extra minutes.

| Recipe says (oven) | Set the air fryer to | Start checking at | Typical small-batch time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 325°F, 40 min (dense cake) | 300°F | 30 min | 25 to 35 min in a 6-inch pan |
| 350°F, 30 min (layer cake) | 325°F | 22 min | 20 to 28 min in a 6-inch pan |
| 350°F, 25 min (brownies) | 325°F | 18 min | 18 to 24 min in a 6-inch pan |
| 350°F, 10 min (cookies) | 325°F | 6 min | 6 to 8 min |
| 375°F, 20 min (muffins) | 350°F | 12 min | 12 to 16 min in cups |
| 375°F, 15 min (dinner rolls) | 350°F | 10 min | 10 to 14 min |
| 400°F, 12 min (biscuits) | 375°F | 8 min | 8 to 11 min |

Times in the last column are for small pans, not the full recipe. If you are cutting a recipe down to fit, our guide to [scaling down recipes for one](/blog/scale-down-recipes-for-one/) shows how to handle the eggs and the spoon math.

### Do You Preheat an Air Fryer for Baking?

Yes. Preheat for 3 to 5 minutes at the baking temperature. A cold start gives you flat cookies and gummy cake bottoms. If your model has no preheat button, run it empty for 3 minutes.

### Check the Real Temperature Once

Air fryer dials are not always accurate. Some run 15 to 25°F hot or cold. Put a small [Taylor 2.5-Inch Dial Oven Thermometer](https://www.amazon.com/Taylor-3506-Precision-Oven-Thermometer/dp/B000BQWMTK/?tag=kitchenpot-20) in the basket, set 325°F, and read it after 10 minutes. Its 2.5-inch dial fits where a big oven thermometer will not. Now you know how far off your machine runs.

## What Pans Fit in an Air Fryer?

Any oven-safe pan works if it fits with room to spare. That includes metal, ceramic and silicone. Use glass only if its maker says it is safe in an air fryer. Leave about half an inch of air around the pan so the hot air can move down its sides.

Measure the flat floor of your basket, not the top rim, because most baskets taper. Subtract about an inch. That is the widest pan you should buy. Then check the height: you want the top of the batter at least 2 inches below the heating coil after it rises.

| Basket size | Floor width (typical) | Pans that fit | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 to 2.6 quarts | about 6 inches | Two 6-ounce custard cups | Single cakes, crisps, 2 to 3 cookies |
| 3.5 to 4 quarts | about 7 to 8 inches | 6-inch round pan, 6-inch cake barrel, three custard cups | Small layer cakes, 4 cookies, 4 rolls |
| 5.5 to 6 quarts | about 8 to 9 inches | 7-inch round pan or cake barrel, two mini loaf pans | Snack cakes, 6 cookies, a small pizza |
| Dual-basket models | each side about 6 to 7 inches | One 6-inch pan or cups per side | Two different bakes at once |

## Can You Bake a Cake in an Air Fryer?

Yes, and a small cake is where an air fryer shines. A 6-inch round holds a little more than half of an 8-inch layer recipe. That is two to four slices, which is right for one or two people.

Here is how we bake a 6-inch cake from an 8-inch recipe.

1. Make half the batter. Grease the pan and line the bottom with a circle of parchment cut to fit.
2. Fill the pan about half to two-thirds full. Any extra batter goes into a custard cup as a bonus mini cake.
3. Preheat the air fryer to 325°F (the recipe said 350°F).
4. Set the pan in the basket and bake for 15 minutes.
5. Look at the top. If it is already golden, lay a piece of foil loosely over the pan. Tuck the edges under the pan rim so the fan cannot lift it.
6. Bake 5 to 10 minutes more. A toothpick in the center should come out with a few dry crumbs.
7. Cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn it out onto a rack.

Lift the pan out with heat-resistant gloves, not a thin towel. A pair like the [Ekogrips Max Heat Silicone Gloves](https://www.amazon.com/Ekogrips-Premium-Heat-Resistant-BBQ-Silicone-Gloves/dp/B00HLPXL80/?tag=kitchenpot-20) lets you grip the pan rim inside the basket, and silicone wipes clean of batter. We compare more options in our guide to [heat-resistant gloves for cooking](/blog/10-best-heat-resistant-gloves-for-cooking/).

### Can You Bake a Small Cake in an Air Fryer?

Yes. For one serving, use a 6-ounce oven-safe ceramic ramekin or custard cup. That size is right for a single molten chocolate cake, a cup of bread pudding or a mini fruit crisp. Pick ceramic or metal over glass, since many glass bakeware makers do not rate their dishes for the direct, close heat of an air fryer.

Fill each cup halfway and bake at 325°F for 10 to 14 minutes. Ceramic needs care: let the cups come to room temperature before they go in. When they come out, set them on a dry towel or trivet, never a cold wet counter.

### Why Air Fryer Cakes Dome and Crack

Doming happens when the top sets before the middle has risen. The fix is the 25°F drop plus foil once the top colors. A pan filled more than two-thirds full also domes, because the batter rises close to the coil. If your cakes still peak, drop another 10°F and bake a few minutes longer.

## Can You Bake Cookies in an Air Fryer?

Yes. Cookies are the easiest air fryer bake. Here is the method for chocolate chip cookies:

1. Scoop dough into balls of about 1 tablespoon each. Chill them for 20 minutes if the dough is soft.
2. Preheat to 325°F (the recipe said 350°F).
3. Set the dough balls about 2 inches apart. Four fit in most 4-quart baskets.
4. Bake 6 to 8 minutes. The edges should be golden and the centers still soft.
5. Leave them in the basket for 2 to 3 minutes before you move them. They firm up as they cool.

Frozen cookie dough works the same way. Add 1 to 2 minutes. Our favorite solo trick: freeze a full batch of dough balls and bake four at a time. Keep them in a freezer bag or one of the [best airtight food storage containers](/blog/best-airtight-food-storage-containers/), and they keep for about 3 months. It is a good way to [cook for one without wasting food](/blog/cook-for-one-without-wasting-food/).

### Can You Bake Cookies in an Air Fryer Without Parchment?

Yes. Spray the basket or crisper plate lightly with oil, then put the dough right on it. Firm doughs work best: chocolate chip, peanut butter, oatmeal and shortbread. Our guide to the [best oil for an air fryer](/blog/best-oil-for-air-fryer/) explains which ones handle the heat.

Very soft dough, like a thin sugar cookie, can sag into the holes of a basket. For those, use a small pan, a silicone liner or perforated parchment.

## Can You Bake Bread in an Air Fryer?

Yes. Quick breads and yeast breads both work, as long as you keep the size small.

**Quick bread** rises with baking soda or baking powder, not yeast. Banana bread, cornbread and muffins all fit here. Bake them in a mini loaf pan at 325°F. A mini banana loaf takes about 20 to 28 minutes. Cover the top with foil after 15 minutes, because the sugar in the batter browns fast.

**Flatbreads and small pizzas** are the fastest bread bakes. A 6-inch pizza on a pizza pan takes about 7 to 9 minutes at 375°F. For leftover slices, our guide on [how to reheat pizza](/blog/how-to-reheat-pizza/) covers that job too.

### Can You Bake Bread in an Air Fryer With Yeast?

Yes. The rise happens outside the air fryer, and the bake happens inside. Here is how we do a batch of four dinner rolls:

1. Make your dough as the recipe says and let it rise once until doubled.
2. Shape four rolls of about 2 ounces each. Set them in a greased 6-inch pan or cake barrel, close but not touching.
3. Cover and let them rise again for 30 to 45 minutes, until puffy.
4. Preheat the air fryer to 350°F (the recipe said 375°F).
5. Brush the tops with egg or milk. Bake 10 to 14 minutes.
6. If the tops get dark before 10 minutes, lay foil over them for the rest of the bake.

The surest way to know bread is done is to check its inside temperature. Soft rolls and enriched breads, which have butter, eggs or milk, are done at about 190°F. Lean breads, like a plain white or crusty loaf, are done at about 200°F to 210°F. A fast instant-read thermometer like the [ThermoWorks ThermoPop 2](https://www.amazon.com/ThermoWorks-ThermoPop-2-Instant-Read-Thermometer/dp/B0DC8FD41B/?tag=kitchenpot-20) gives you a reading in a few seconds, so the basket is not open long.

Bread dough is also where weighing helps most. Two-ounce rolls are hard to eyeball. A small, flat scale like the [Escali Primo Digital Kitchen Scale](https://www.amazon.com/Escali-Primo-Digital-Kitchen-Scale/dp/B0007GAWNW/?tag=kitchenpot-20) slides into a drawer and weighs up to 11 pounds. Ready for bigger loaves? See our guide to [bread proofing baskets](/blog/best-bread-proofing-basket-reviews/).

One limit: a full 9 x 5 sandwich loaf is too tall for most baskets. The top sits too close to the coil and burns before the middle bakes. Stick to rolls, mini loaves and flat shapes.

## Air Fryer vs Oven: How the Results Compare

Here is how the results compare for small batches.

| What you notice | Air fryer | Regular oven |
|---|---|---|
| Preheat time | 3 to 5 minutes | 10 to 15 minutes |
| Bake time | About 20 to 25 percent shorter | As the recipe says |
| Top crust | Browns fast; can dome or crack | Even browning |
| Cookie texture | Crisp edges, soft centers | Even crispness across the tray |
| Cake crumb | Slightly drier edges | Even, moist crumb |
| Batch size | 4 cookies, one 6-inch cake | Two full trays or a 9 x 13 pan |
| Heat in the kitchen | Very little | Warms the whole room |
| Energy for one small pan | Low | High for the amount of food |

Our verdict: for one to four servings, the air fryer wins on speed, energy and heat. For a birthday cake, a full loaf or holiday cookies, use the oven. ## Parchment, Foil and Silicone: What Is Safe in the Basket

These three liners all work, but each has one rule you must follow.

**Parchment paper.** Never put parchment in the basket without food on it, and never preheat with loose parchment inside. The fan lifts it into the heating coil, where it can scorch or catch fire. Put the dough or food on the paper first, then place it in the preheated basket.

**Foil.** Foil is safe if it is weighed down or tucked in. Use it to cover a cake top or to make a sling, a folded strip under a pan that helps you lift it out. Never cover the whole basket floor with it. That blocks the air flow and makes the bottom of your bake pale and soggy.

**Silicone.** Silicone liners and cups are safe at normal baking heat and do not fly around. Check that yours is rated for at least the top heat you bake at.

Two more rules. Do not use a pan that touches the heating coil. And do not use plastic or paper plates, which melt or burn.

## Where to Put the Air Fryer When You Bake

An air fryer gets hot and pushes hot air out of a vent at the back. Bake with it on an open counter, with several inches of clear space on every side. Follow the clearance in your manual.

Never run it inside a cabinet, on a lower shelf of a rolling cart, or tucked under the upper cabinets. The steam and heat damage the cabinet bottoms and can trap heat around the machine. If it lives under the cabinets, slide it out onto open counter before you turn it on.

## Common Mistakes When Baking in an Air Fryer

- **Using the oven temperature.** The top burns and the middle stays raw. Always drop about 25°F.
- **Skipping the preheat.** Cookies spread flat and cakes get dense bottoms. Preheat for 3 to 5 minutes.
- **Filling the pan too full.** The batter rises into the coil zone and burns. Fill pans half to two-thirds full.
- **Using a pan that fits too tightly.** No air flows down the sides, so the edges stay pale. Leave half an inch all around.
- **Preheating with parchment inside.** The fan throws it into the coil. Add parchment only with food on it.
- **Opening the basket every minute.** Each time, the heat drops. Check once at the three-quarter mark, then every 2 to 3 minutes.
- **Baking a full-size recipe.** Too much batter in a deep pan bakes unevenly. Cut the recipe to fit.
- **Running it under the cabinets.** Bake on an open counter with air all around it.
