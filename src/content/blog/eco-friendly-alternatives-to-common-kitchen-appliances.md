Going greener in the kitchen does not mean throwing everything out. Many appliances on your counter have a simpler, lower-impact alternative.

Often that alternative is smaller, cheaper and lasts longer too. Here are seven swaps worth making, and when to keep the appliance instead.

**The short version:** Replace a drip coffee maker with a [Bodum Chambord French press](https://www.amazon.com/Bodum-Chambord-French-Press-Coffee-Maker/dp/B00008XEWG/?tag=kitchenpot-20) or a [Hario V60 pour-over dripper](https://www.amazon.com/Hario-V60-Ceramic-Coffee-Dripper/dp/B000P4D5HG/?tag=kitchenpot-20). Swap single-use bags for [Stasher silicone bags](https://www.amazon.com/Stasher-Silicone-Reusable-Sandwich-Bag-Clear/dp/B01DZQT9CU/?tag=kitchenpot-20). Keep scraps out of the trash with the [OXO Easy-Clean Compost Bin](https://www.amazon.com/OXO-Good-Grips-Easy-Clean-Compost/dp/B07ZDRTFYN/?tag=kitchenpot-20), the easiest countertop bin to live with.

## What Makes a Swap Eco-Friendly?

A good swap does at least one of these:

- **Uses less energy.** No warming plate, no standby power, no motor.
- **Lasts longer.** Fewer electronics means fewer parts to fail.
- **Cuts waste.** Replaces paper filters, plastic bags or food waste in the trash.
- **Takes less space.** In a small kitchen, that matters too.

The greenest product is often the one you already own. Use your current appliances until they wear out, then replace them with a lower-impact option.

## A Simple Coffee Routine Without a Coffee Maker

If you have only ever used a drip machine, a manual routine sounds like more work. In practice, it adds about two minutes.

1. Heat water in a kettle to just off the boil, about 200°F.
2. Grind or measure your coffee: roughly 2 tablespoons per 6-ounce cup.
3. For a French press, add the coffee and water, stir, wait four minutes and press slowly.
4. For a pour-over, wet the filter, add the coffee, pour a little water to let it bloom for 30 seconds, then pour the rest in slow circles.

Fresh-ground coffee makes the biggest difference to taste with either method. If you would rather keep one appliance for coffee, a [coffee maker with a grinder](/blog/best-coffee-maker-with-a-grinder/) is a good middle ground. For something different, try [bulletproof coffee without a blender](/blog/how-to-make-bulletproof-coffee-without-blender/).

## Living Without a Garbage Disposal

Many older apartments have no disposal, and a compost bin makes that easy. Scrape plates into the bin, not the sink, and use a sink strainer to catch the rest.

Keep the bin lidded and empty it often, especially in summer, to avoid fruit flies. Our guide on [how to keep pests out of a small kitchen](/blog/how-to-keep-pests-out-of-a-small-kitchen/) covers what to do if they appear.

Not everything belongs in a home compost bin. Meat, bones, dairy and oily food attract pests in a backyard pile, though many city green-bin programs accept them. Check what your local service takes.

## When the Appliance Is the Greener Choice

Not every manual swap saves energy. In some cases, the appliance wins.

- **Dishwasher vs hand washing.** A full, efficient dishwasher often uses less water than washing by hand under a running tap. See [best compact Energy Star appliances](/blog/best-compact-energy-star-appliances-for-small-kitchens/).
- **Electric kettle vs gas stove kettle.** An electric kettle wastes less heat than a kettle on a gas flame.
- **Microwave vs oven.** Reheating in a microwave uses far less energy than heating the oven.
- **Toaster oven vs full oven.** For small meals, a toaster oven heats a fraction of the space and is ready in minutes.

The goal is lower total impact, not avoiding plugs for their own sake.

## More Swaps That Cost Nothing

- **Air dry dishes** instead of using heated dry.
- **Use a clean towel** instead of paper towels for drying hands and produce.
- **Thaw food in the fridge** overnight instead of in the microwave.
- **Batch cook** so you heat the oven or pot once for several meals.
- **Boil only the water you need** in the kettle.
- **Cut vegetables smaller** so they cook faster.

## Greener Cooking With the Right Appliances

When an appliance does need replacing, choose a small, efficient one. Induction burners, toaster ovens and small air fryers use less energy than a full stove and oven for small meals.
