If you want to know how to organize a corner cabinet, start here. Corners hide the most space in a small kitchen, and most of it sits where your arm cannot reach.

The good news is that every corner cabinet can work. The trick is to match the fix to the type of corner you have. Below we show you how to tell which type you own, what to buy for it, and what to store inside so you stop crawling in on your knees.

**The short version:** First, figure out your corner type: a lazy susan corner with an L-shaped door, a blind corner where one cabinet hides behind another, or an upper corner. For lazy susan corners, add or replace the spinning shelves. For blind corners, use a pull-out that brings the back section to the door, or long bins with handles if you rent. In every corner, keep daily items in the front and rarely used items in the back.

## Why Corner Cabinets Are So Hard to Use

A corner is where two runs of cabinets meet at a right angle. Standard base cabinets are 24 inches deep, so the corner box ends up much deeper than a normal cabinet. The back of it can sit 30 inches or more from your hand.

That creates three problems:

- **You cannot see the back.** Anything past arm's length disappears, so you buy a second can of beans or a second muffin pan.
- **You cannot reach the back.** You have to kneel, lean in and pull out the front row to get one thing.
- **Things fall in a heap.** With no structure, pots and bowls tip into the gap and jam.

In a small kitchen with only a few base cabinets, that corner is a big share of your storage. Leaving it as a black hole is a real loss. Our wider guide to [small kitchen cabinet organization ideas](/blog/small-kitchen-cabinet-organization-ideas/) covers the other cabinets. This one is corner-only.

## Know Your Corner Cabinet Type First

Open the door and look before you buy anything. Corner cabinets come in four common types, and each one needs a different fix.

### Lazy Susan Corner (L-Shaped Base)

This is a square cabinet that fills the corner, with a door on each wall. The two doors are joined, either hinged together like a book (a bi-fold door) or fixed at an angle and attached to the shelves (a pie-cut door). Most measure 33 or 36 inches along each wall. If yours has round spinning shelves, you already have a lazy susan. If it has flat fixed shelves, it is built for one.

### Blind Corner (Base)

Here one cabinet runs straight into the corner, and the cabinet on the other wall butts up against its side. The front of the corner section is covered by a filler panel, so it is "blind." You get one normal door, and the space behind the neighboring cabinet is hidden. This is the most common corner in rentals and the hardest to use.

### Upper Corner Cabinet

Upper cabinets are 12 inches deep, so an upper corner is smaller. Most are either a diagonal cabinet with an angled door, about 24 inches along each wall, or a blind upper corner with a hidden end. They sit about 18 inches above the counter, so the top shelf is usually out of reach.

### Deep Corner Pantry or Tall Corner

Some kitchens have a tall corner pantry with a diagonal door. It is deep and triangular in the back. It works like a lazy susan corner stretched upward, and the same turntable rules apply. Our [small pantry organization ideas](/blog/small-pantry-organization-ideas/) cover the shelf layout.

| Corner type | How to spot it | Best fix | Renter-safe fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lazy susan corner, bi-fold door | Two doors hinged together, open flat | Full-circle lazy susan | Large freestanding turntables |
| Lazy susan corner, pie-cut door | Doors fixed at 90 degrees, spin with shelves | Kidney or pie-cut lazy susan | Large freestanding turntables |
| Blind base corner | One door, hidden space to the side | Blind corner pull-out | Long bins with handles plus a turntable |
| Upper diagonal corner | Angled door, triangle shelves | Turntable on each shelf | Same, it needs no drilling |
| Upper blind corner | One door, hidden end | Bins with handles and a riser | Same, it needs no drilling |

## Measure Before You Buy Anything

The opening, not the cabinet, decides what fits. A corner can be huge inside and still have a narrow door. Take these numbers with a tape measure and write them down.

1. **Door opening width.** Measure the clear gap between the face frame edges, not the door. This is the number that decides which pull-outs fit.
2. **Opening height.** Measure from the cabinet floor to the underside of the top frame.
3. **Interior depth.** Measure from the back of the face frame to the back wall, on both sides of a corner.
4. **Hinge position.** Note which side the hinges are on. Hinges and the door itself can block a pull-out from swinging.
5. **Obstacles.** Look for pipes, a dishwasher drain line or a center post. Under a corner sink, pipes change the whole plan.
6. **Shelf height.** Measure the gap between shelves if you plan to add risers or stacked bins.

For a freestanding turntable, leave at least an inch of clearance all around the turntable so it spins without catching the frame. For any built-in pull-out, check the maker's minimum opening width against your measurement before you order.

## How to Organize a Blind Corner Cabinet

A blind corner is the toughest corner, so start here if you have one. You have three levels of fix, from a full install to a cheap renter setup.

### Best Fix: A Blind Corner Pull-Out

A blind corner pull-out is a set of shelves or baskets on a hinged arm. You open the door, pull the front baskets out, and the hidden back baskets slide over into the opening. Nothing stays buried.

For most blind corners, we recommend the [Rev-A-Shelf Blind Corner 2-Tier Pullout](https://www.amazon.com/Rev-A-Shelf-5PSP-18-CR-Blind-Corner-Pullout/dp/B00H4Q79CU/?tag=kitchenpot-20). It has two tiers with four adjustable chrome wire baskets, and it needs a door opening of at least 18 inches. It screws to the cabinet floor and side, so it suits owners or renters with a landlord's okay.

If your opening is narrower, the [Rev-A-Shelf Half Moon 2-Shelf Pullout](https://www.amazon.com/Rev-A-Shelf-Half-Moon-Blind-Corner-Shelves/dp/B0009EJXHI/?tag=kitchenpot-20) needs only about 13.25 inches. Its two half-moon shelves pivot and slide out through the door. It costs less than the four-basket unit and suits pots, bowls and small appliances you use now and then.

### Mid Fix: A Slide-Out Drawer in the Front

Sometimes a pull-out will not fit because of pipes or a center stile (the wood strip between doors). Then put a slide-out drawer in the front section and use the back for storage you rarely touch.

The [Lynk Professional Pull Out Cabinet Organizer](https://www.amazon.com/Lynk-Professional-Pull-Out-Cabinet-Organizer/dp/B000SOYK9U/?tag=kitchenpot-20) is a single-tier wire drawer, 11 inches wide and 21 inches deep. It screws to the cabinet floor and runs on full slides, so the back of the drawer comes right out to you. It is great for a stack of skillets or lids.

### Renter Fix: Bins With Handles

If you cannot drill, make the back section reachable with long bins. A bin with a front handle works like a drawer with no hardware. Pull the bin, grab what you need, push it back.

We like the [iDesign Linus Stacking Organizer Bin, Extra Large](https://www.amazon.com/iDesign-Linus-Stacking-Organizer-Bin-Extra-Large/dp/B0019HJMRA/?tag=kitchenpot-20) for the hidden back section. It is clear, so you see what is inside, and it is long enough to fill the dead space. For lighter loads, the [mDesign Ligne Stackable Storage Bins with Handles](https://www.amazon.com/mDesign-Ligne-Stackable-Storage-Bin-4-Pack/dp/B07NQLHNQ4/?tag=kitchenpot-20) come in a 4-pack with built-in handles.

Then set the front section up for daily use. The [AmzAge No-Drill Pull Out Cabinet Organizer](https://www.amazon.com/AmzAge-No-Drill-Pull-Out-Cabinet-Organizer/dp/B0DJ7J7LGY/?tag=kitchenpot-20) mounts with adhesive, so there are no screw holes. It is a small mesh basket, about 9.6 inches wide and 11.8 inches deep, so treat it as a drawer for wraps, lids or cleaning cloths.

### The Blind Corner Load Plan

Here is how we would load a blind corner with no pull-out:

1. Put the longest bin at the far back of the blind side. Fill it with holiday dishes or backup paper goods.
2. Put a second bin in front of it with things you use monthly, like a roasting pan or cake pans.
3. Leave the area right behind the door for weekly items, like a stockpot or mixing bowls.
4. Turn every bin so its handle faces the door. Add a label on the handle end.
5. Keep a clear lane on the floor so a bin can slide out without lifting the others.

## Corner Cabinet Lazy Susan: Is It Worth It?

A corner cabinet lazy susan is worth it in a true L-shaped corner. It turns a dead triangle into two round shelves you can spin to bring anything to the door. In that cabinet, it is the single best fix.

It is a poor fit in a blind corner. The narrow door blocks most of the circle, so you only reach the slice in front of you. Use a pull-out or bins there instead.

### Pick the Right Shape for Your Door

The shape depends on your door, so look at it first.

- **Bi-fold door (doors fold open on a hinge):** use a full-circle lazy susan. The [Rev-A-Shelf 28-Inch Full-Circle 2-Tier Lazy Susan](https://www.amazon.com/Rev-A-Shelf-Full-Circle-2-Tier-Lazy-Susan/dp/B00LPB5OBI/?tag=kitchenpot-20) has two white shelves on a center post that spin all the way around.
- **Pie-cut door (doors attached to the shelves at 90 degrees):** use a shape with a notch so the door can ride on it. The [Rev-A-Shelf Kidney-Shaped 2-Shelf Lazy Susan](https://www.amazon.com/Rev-A-Shelf-Kidney-2-Shelf-Lazy-Susan/dp/B008BNKK62/?tag=kitchenpot-20) has a kidney shape with a cutout that clears the corner of the door.

Both mount on a center post and need a few screws. Measure the inside of the cabinet on both walls and check the size before you order. If your old lazy susan just wobbles, replacing the shelves is often a one-hour job.

### Fix a Lazy Susan That Keeps Jamming

Most jams have simple causes:

- **Items hang over the edge.** Anything that sticks out catches the frame. Keep everything inside the shelf lip.
- **Too much weight on one side.** Spread heavy pots around the circle, not in one spot.
- **Tall items on the top shelf.** They hit the cabinet top or the shelf above. Measure the clearance and store tall things on the bottom tier.
- **Loose center post.** Tighten the screws at the top and bottom of the post.

## How to Organize a Corner Cabinet Without a Lazy Susan

If you cannot install a built-in lazy susan, freestanding turntables do much of the same job. They sit on the shelf with no screws, so they are fine for renters.

For the middle of a corner shelf, use the biggest turntable that spins freely. The [YouCopia SmoothSpin Two-Tier Turntable](https://www.amazon.com/YouCopia-SmoothSpin-Two-Tier-Turntable-Organizer/dp/B0GSCNL52L/?tag=kitchenpot-20) gives you two levels in one footprint, which suits jars, oils and cans. If you want sections, the [YouCopia SmoothSpin Turntable With 3 Bins](https://www.amazon.com/YouCopia-SmoothSpin-Turntable-3-Bins-Organizer/dp/B0GSDNDQ64/?tag=kitchenpot-20) has removable bins you can lift out and carry to the counter.

For a cheap fix, the [Copco Basics Non-Skid Lazy Susan Turntable, 12-Inch](https://www.amazon.com/Copco-Basics-Non-Skid-Cabinet-Turntable-12-Inch/dp/B0036OQWTU/?tag=kitchenpot-20) is a simple 12-inch disc with a grippy surface. Use two of them side by side on a wide corner shelf.

For long, narrow shelves, a round turntable wastes the corners. The [YouCopia MagicTrack Rectangle Turntable](https://www.amazon.com/YouCopia-MagicTrack-Rectangle-Lazy-Susan-Turntable/dp/B0FQXRMSBD/?tag=kitchenpot-20) slides and spins in a rectangular footprint, so it fills more of a deep shelf.

### Our Turntable Rules

- Measure the shelf depth and subtract at least an inch for clearance before you pick a size.
- Group by use: all oils on one turntable, all baking items on another.
- Put the tallest items in the center and the shortest at the edge so you can see over them.
- Do not overload a turntable with heavy cast iron. Use a shelf or a pull-out for heavy pans.

## How to Organize a Deep Corner Cabinet

A deep corner is any cabinet where the back is more than an arm's length away. The method is the same whether it is a base corner, a corner pantry or a deep blind corner.

Our rule of thumb is to split the cabinet into zones by reach:

| Zone | Distance from the door | What goes there |
|---|---|---|
| Front zone | First 12 inches | Items you use weekly: stockpot, mixing bowls, colander |
| Middle zone | 12 to 24 inches | Items you use monthly: roasting pan, cake pans, slow cooker |
| Back zone | Past 24 inches | Items you use a few times a year: holiday platters, extra paper towels, a turkey roaster |

Then give each zone a way to come forward. Use a turntable in the middle, bins with handles in the back, and keep the front open for grab-and-go items.

Two extra tricks help a lot:

- **Add a second level.** Most base cabinets have one shelf and a big gap above each level. A riser doubles the usable surface. The [YouCopia UpSpace Adjustable-Height Shelf Riser](https://www.amazon.com/YouCopia-UpSpace-Adjustable-Height-Shelf-Riser/dp/B07XVQWCS5/?tag=kitchenpot-20) is 16 inches wide and 9 inches deep, with legs that adjust to your stack height. For a budget pair, the [GEDLIRE Metal Wire Cabinet Shelf Organizer, Set of 2](https://www.amazon.com/GEDLIRE-Cabinet-Shelf-Organizer-Set-of-2/dp/B098SXMTWP/?tag=kitchenpot-20) gives you two 13-by-9.4-inch wire shelves.
- **Light the back.** A dark corner is a forgotten corner. The [Mr Beams Slim LED Motion Light](https://www.amazon.com/Mr-Beams-MB850-Wireless-Slim-LED-Motion-Light/dp/B008UPDPIG/?tag=kitchenpot-20) runs on batteries and sticks inside the cabinet. ## What Should You Store in a Corner Cabinet?

Store things you use weekly or less. Corners are slow to reach even when organized, so do not make them hold your daily plates or your favorite pan.

Good corner items:

- A stockpot, Dutch oven or pasta pot
- Mixing bowls, nested
- A roasting pan, sheet pans and cake pans
- A slow cooker, stand mixer bowl or food processor you use now and then
- Backup pantry stock: extra cans, oil, rice and flour in sealed containers
- Holiday platters, serving bowls and a gravy boat
- Paper towels and napkins in bulk

Bad corner items:

- Daily dishes, mugs and glasses (keep them in the easiest upper cabinet)
- Your everyday skillet
- Spices you cook with often
- Anything you need while food is on the stove

For heavier gear, our guide on how to [store pots and pans in a small kitchen](/blog/store-pots-and-pans-in-a-small-kitchen/) explains where each piece belongs. For appliances, see how to [store small appliances in a small kitchen](/blog/store-small-appliances-in-a-small-kitchen/). Store any appliance in the corner unplugged and cool. Never run a slow cooker, air fryer or toaster oven inside a closed cabinet.

Keep dry food in sealed, clear containers in the corner. Open bags in a dark back corner draw pests. ## Organizing Pots, Pans and Lids in a Corner

Pots and pans are the most common corner contents, and the most likely to jam. The fix is to store them on edge or in a single layer, not in a tall pile.

In the front section of a blind corner, stand pans upright in a rack. That lets it fit the front of most base cabinets. You slide one pan out without lifting the others.

On a lazy susan, nest pots by size and set the lids upside down on top. A lid flipped over sits flat and stays put when the shelf spins. Our guide to [organizing pot lids in a small kitchen](/blog/how-to-organize-pot-lids-in-a-small-kitchen/) has more lid fixes.

## How to Organize a Top Corner Cabinet

Upper corners are shallow, high and often triangular. The two problems are the back corner and the top shelf.

### Use a Turntable on Every Shelf

On a diagonal upper corner, the back of the triangle is out of sight. A turntable fixes it. The [YouCopia MagicTrack Spice Turntable 2-Tier](https://www.amazon.com/YouCopia-MagicTrack-Spice-Turntable-2-Tier/dp/B0FQXT71VZ/?tag=kitchenpot-20) is small enough for a 12-inch-deep shelf and holds spice jars on two levels. Spin it and every label comes to the front.

### Split the Tall Gap

Upper shelves often have 12 inches or more of air above a stack of mugs. A corner shelf fills it. The [LUMAMU 2-Tier Kitchen Corner Shelf](https://www.amazon.com/LUMAMU-2-Tier-Kitchen-Corner-Shelf/dp/B0B5SX17XS/?tag=kitchenpot-20) is shaped to sit in a corner, so it uses the back point of the shelf for small plates or bowls.

### Keep the Top Shelf Light

Put light, rarely used things on the top shelf: a cake stand, a pitcher, extra glasses for guests. Never put heavy dishes or glass bakeware up high. They are hard to lift down safely from overhead.

Get a real step stool, not a chair. The [COSCO 2-Step Big Step Folding Step Stool](https://www.amazon.com/Cosco-2-Step-Big-Step-Folding-Stool/dp/B0032JRVPI/?tag=kitchenpot-20) has two wide steps and folds to about 2 inches flat. It slides into the gap beside the fridge.

### Upper Corner Cheat Sheet

| Shelf | What to store | Tool |
|---|---|---|
| Bottom shelf (easiest) | Spices, oils, everyday mugs | Two-tier spice turntable |
| Middle shelf | Small bowls, tea, coffee | Corner shelf or riser |
| Top shelf | Guest glasses, cake stand, pitcher | Nothing heavy; use a step stool |

Here is how the main ways to bring a corner out to you compare.

## Corner Sink Cabinets

Some small kitchens put the sink in the corner. Then the corner base holds a drain, a trap (the U-shaped pipe) and often a disposal. A lazy susan or blind corner pull-out usually will not fit around them.

Instead, work around the pipes. Use a U-shaped or L-shaped shelf that wraps the drain, and put bins on either side. Keep cleaning supplies in a caddy you can lift out. Our full guide to how to [organize under the kitchen sink](/blog/organize-under-the-kitchen-sink/) covers corner sinks, pipe-friendly shelves and leak trays.

Never store food or food containers under a sink. A slow leak can soak them without you noticing.

## Common Mistakes With Corner Cabinets

These are the mistakes we see most often:

- **Buying before measuring.** A blind corner pull-out that is one inch too wide for the opening is useless. Measure the clear opening, not the door.
- **Putting a round lazy susan in a blind corner.** You will only reach the slice behind the door. Use a pull-out or bins.
- **Storing daily items in the back.** If you reach for it every day, it does not belong in a corner.
- **Stacking pans in a tower.** The pan you need is always at the bottom. Store them upright or one layer deep.
- **Heavy items on high corner shelves.** Lifting a heavy dish down from overhead at an angle is how things fall.
- **Overloading a spinning shelf.** Too much weight on one side makes it drag and jam.

## A One-Afternoon Corner Cabinet Reset

Here is how we would reset a messy corner cabinet in about two hours:

1. **Empty it completely.** Put everything on the counter or a table.
2. **Wipe it out.** Check for leaks, crumbs and pest signs, especially in the far back.
3. **Measure.** Take the opening width, height and depth on both walls.
4. **Sort into three piles.** Weekly use, monthly use and a few times a year.
5. **Cut what you do not use.** Donate the third mixing bowl set and the pan you never reach for.
6. **Pick your system.** Pull-out, lazy susan, turntables or bins, based on your corner type.
7. **Load back to front.** Rare items go in the back, weekly items in the front.
8. **Label bins on the handle end.** You should read every label from the door.

If your corner is overflowing even after a reset, the problem is too much stuff, not the cabinet. ## When to Change the Corner Itself

Sometimes no organizer fixes a bad corner. If you are remodeling, you have better choices.

- **Corner drawers.** These are angled drawers that meet in a point. They use the corner well and pull all the way out.
- **A real lazy susan corner instead of a blind corner.** It takes the same space and is much easier to use.
- **Open corner shelves on the wall.** In a tiny kitchen, open shelves in the upper corner can feel lighter than a closed box.
