## What BTUs Actually Mean on a Gas Cooktop

BTU stands for British thermal unit. On a cooktop, it tells you how much heat a burner puts out in an hour at full flame. More BTU means more heat, faster.

But the top number is only half the story. A great cooktop has a wide spread from high to low.

| Burner size | What it does well | Example on this list |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15,000 to 20,000 BTU | Boiling big pots, searing, stir-frying | Café 20,000; Empava 36GC61-GX 20,000 |
| 9,000 to 12,000 BTU | Everyday frying, sautéing, steaming | GE 12,000; Cosmo 12,000 |
| 6,000 to 8,000 BTU | Eggs, small pans, reheating | Empava 36GC61-GX 8,000 |
| 5,000 BTU or less | Simmering sauces, melting, keeping warm | Empava 30GC5B70C 3,400; Gasland 4,000 |

One burner of 15,000 BTU or more covers boiling and searing. For the low end, look for 5,000 BTU or under. If yours runs hotter, a flat heat diffuser plate under the pot helps.

Strong burners need the right pans to make use of them. A thick-based [sauté pan](/blog/best-saute-pan/) spreads high heat evenly, and our guide to [choosing energy-efficient cookware](/blog/how-to-choose-energy-efficient-cookware-for-a-small-kitchen/) explains why a flat base matching the flame size wastes less gas.

## Sealed Burners: Why Every Pick Has Them

A sealed burner has its base closed off from the inside of the cooktop. Spills stay on the top surface instead of dripping down into the burner box.

Every cooktop on this list uses them. To keep them burning well, wipe spills once the cooktop is cool. Lift off the burner caps now and then and clear the small ports with a pin or soft brush. A weak or yellow flame usually means a blocked port, the same problem covered in [why a gas grill has a weak flame](/blog/why-your-gas-grill-has-a-weak-flame/).

## Continuous Grates vs Separate Grates

Continuous grates form one flat surface across the cooktop. You can slide a heavy pot from burner to burner without lifting it. The GE, Café and both Cosmo picks use them.

Separate grates are easier to fit in the sink, but you lift pans over the gaps.

Heavy cast iron grates hold steady under a [cast iron skillet](/blog/lancaster-cast-iron-review/). Dishwasher-safe grates, like the GE and Café ones, save the most cleaning.

A [tea kettle made for gas stoves](/blog/best-tea-kettle-for-gas-stove/) with a wide, flat base sits steady on any of these grates.

## Cooktop Top Materials: Stainless, Enamel or Glass

The surface under the grates decides how the cooktop looks after a year of cooking.

Stainless steel is the classic choice but shows fingerprints. Black enamel hides stains but can chip. Gas on glass wipes clean easily but can crack from a hard knock.

Most picks here use stainless steel. Heat can leave rainbow marks on stainless near the burners, which is harmless, as explained in [why stainless steel turns rainbow](/blog/why-does-stainless-steel-turn-rainbow/). The same cleaning tips from our guide on [how to clean stainless steel pans](/blog/how-to-clean-stainless-steel-pans/) work on a stainless cooktop.

## Flame-Failure Safety: The Feature Few People Check

A flame-failure device, also called a thermocouple, is a small metal probe beside each burner. While the flame burns, it stays hot and keeps the gas valve open. If the flame goes out, the probe cools and the valve closes within seconds.

Both Gasland picks list this cutoff on every burner. If you have young children, cook in a drafty spot, or plan to put a cooktop in an RV or tiny home, put this feature high on your list.

After lighting, hold the knob in for a few seconds so the probe can heat up.

## Gas vs Induction Cooktop

Both are good. They suit different cooks.

| | Gas | Induction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How it heats | Open flame under the pan | Magnetic field heats the pan itself |
| Speed | Fast | Faster |
| Pans | Any pan, including round-bottom woks | Magnetic pans only |
| Power hookup | Gas line plus a 120V outlet | Usually a 240V circuit |
| Kitchen heat | Warms the room | Keeps the room cooler |
| Ventilation | Needs good venting for combustion gases | Venting for cooking smoke only |

## 30-Inch vs 36-Inch Gas Cooktop

A 30-inch cooktop is the standard and fits a regular base cabinet. A 36-inch model adds room for a fifth or sixth burner, so two big pans fit side by side.

Choose 36 inches if you cook for a crowd often and your counter run is long. Stay at 30 inches if counter space is tight, which is common in [galley kitchens](/blog/galley-kitchen-ideas/) and condos. ### Can You Install a Gas Cooktop Yourself?

Setting the cooktop in the counter is DIY work. The gas hookup needs a licensed gas fitter or plumber, who also leak-tests every joint.

You also need a 120-volt outlet in the cabinet below for the igniters. Plan a shutoff valve within easy reach. While the work is done, our tips on [how to cook without a stove](/blog/how-to-cook-without-a-stove/) can keep meals going for a day or two.

Renters need the landlord's written OK before any gas work.

## Converting a Gas Cooktop to Propane (LP)

Most cooktops ship set for natural gas. Propane runs at a higher pressure, so the burners need smaller jets, called orifices, and the regulator must be set for LP.

## Ventilation: A Gas Cooktop Needs a Hood

Burning gas makes heat, moisture and combustion gases along with smoke, and a hood pulls them out.

A ducted hood that vents outside works best with gas. For a 30-inch cooktop, a 30-inch [under-cabinet range hood](/blog/best-under-cabinet-range-hood/) is the usual fit, and a 36-inch cooktop wants a 36-inch hood. If you have no duct to the outside, a [ductless range hood](/blog/6-best-ductless-range-hood/) filters grease and smells, but it does not remove combustion gases, so open a window too.

Run the hood before and after cooking. Our [small kitchen guide to range hood ventilation](/blog/small-kitchen-guide-range-hood-ventilation-cooking-smell/) explains how to size a hood. Clean the filter often, using the schedule in [how often to clean a range hood filter](/blog/how-often-to-clean-a-range-hood-filter/), because a clogged filter cuts airflow.

### What About a Gas Cooktop With Downdraft?

A downdraft vent pulls smoke down through a slot by the burners. It struggles with tall pots and strong flames, because hot air rises, so an island hood usually works better. Our [small kitchen island ideas](/blog/small-kitchen-island-ideas/) cover how to plan cooking on an island.
