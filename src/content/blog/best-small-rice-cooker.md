## How Much Rice Does a 3-Cup Rice Cooker Make?

About 6 cups of cooked white rice when it is full. The "3 cups" is the dry rice it holds, measured in rice cooker cups.

A rice cooker cup holds 180 ml. A US measuring cup holds about 240 ml. So a rice cooker cup is roughly three quarters of a US cup, and the water lines inside the pot are drawn for that smaller cup.

Measuring with a kitchen cup and filling to the line gives mushy or crunchy rice, so always use the cup that came in the box.

| Dry rice (rice cooker cups) | Cooked rice, roughly | Feeds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.5 cup | 1 cup | 1 small bowl |
| 1 cup | 2 cups | 2 bowls, or 1 person with leftovers |
| 2 cups | 4 cups | 3 to 4 bowls |
| 3 cups | 6 cups | 5 to 6 bowls |

Brown rice, mixed grains and porridge swell more and foam up, so the maximum is lower. Most small cookers mark a lower line for those, often around 2 cups.

## Fuzzy Logic vs Induction vs Pressure vs Basic

The heating type decides how good the rice is, how long it takes and what else the cooker can do. Here is what each one means in plain words.

### Basic (One Touch)

A heating plate sits under the pot. A thermostat clicks the cooker to keep-warm when the pot gets hotter than boiling, which only happens once the water is gone. It is simple, fast and cheap. White rice comes out well. Brown rice and tiny batches are hit-and-miss.

### Fuzzy Logic (Micom)

There is still a plate underneath, but a small chip controls it. It soaks the rice, raises the heat in stages, and adjusts if the pot has more or less in it. The result is more even rice, real brown rice and porridge programs, and a timer. It takes longer than a basic cooker.

### Induction Heating (IH)

Instead of a plate, a coil makes the metal pot itself get hot, all over. Heat reaches the rice from the sides as well as the bottom. That gives the most even cooking, especially for brown rice and full pots.

### Pressure

A sealed lid traps steam, which raises the temperature inside the pot above normal boiling. Water gets into the grain faster, so rice turns softer, stickier and glossier, and brown rice cooks noticeably better. The same idea drives an electric pressure cooker, which our [Instant Pot vs pressure cooker](/blog/instant-pot-vs-pressure-cooker/) comparison explains.

| Type | Rice quality | Speed | Brown rice | Typical price band |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | Good for white rice | Fastest | Firm | Under $50 |
| Fuzzy logic | Very good and even | Slower | Good | About $100 to $200 |
| Induction | Excellent | Medium | Very good | $200 and up |
| Pressure plus induction | Best and stickiest | Fast for its quality | Best | $300 and up |

## Is a Mini Rice Cooker Worth It?

Yes, if you eat rice at least once a week. A rice cooker does not need watching, never boils over onto the stove, and holds rice warm while you finish the rest of the meal.

It also frees a burner. In a kitchen with two burners, or none, that is a real gain. A small cooker uses little power too, which is why rice cookers show up on our list of the [most energy-efficient small kitchen appliances](/blog/most-energy-efficient-small-kitchen-appliances/).

If you only cook rice once a month, a small saucepan with a tight lid is fine. Our guide to [cookware sizes for cooking for one](/blog/best-cookware-sizes-for-cooking-for-one/) covers the right pot.

## Keep-Warm: How Long Can Rice Sit?

Keep-warm holds cooked rice at a safe temperature, well above the danger zone where bacteria grow. That is the real food-safety job of the feature.

Quality is a different story. On a basic cooker, rice dries out and forms a crust after a few hours. Fuzzy logic and induction cookers hold rice much better because they keep the heat lower and steadier. Even so, rice tastes best within about 12 hours.

For anything longer, cool the rice and refrigerate it within two hours, or freeze it. Our guide on [how long leftovers last](/blog/how-long-do-leftovers-last/) gives the fridge timings, and flat portions in [airtight food storage containers](/blog/best-airtight-food-storage-containers/) freeze and reheat fastest.

## Footprint and Storage in a Small Kitchen

Most 3-cup cookers take up a square about 10 inches on each side. That is roughly the space of a dinner plate.

Measure the gap under your upper cabinets before you buy. Clamshell lids, like the Zojirushi and Cuckoo pressure models, open upward and need clearance above. They also push steam out of the top vent, so never run them right under a cabinet door. Slide the cooker forward while it cooks.

Pot-style cookers with glass lids, like the Aroma and the Cuckoo CR-0301C, need less height when closed. A cooker that lives on a shelf frees the counter, which our tips to [make more counter space in a small kitchen](/blog/make-more-counter-space-in-a-small-kitchen/) build on.

## How to Clean a Small Rice Cooker

Cleaning takes a minute if you do it right away. Rice that dries in the pot takes much longer.

1. Unplug the cooker and let it cool.
2. Fill the inner pot with warm water and a drop of dish soap. Let it sit for a few minutes.
3. Wash it with a soft sponge. Never use steel wool on a nonstick pot.
4. Wipe the inner lid. Remove it if your model allows, like the Cuckoo CRP-MHTR0309F.
5. Empty and dry the steam vent cap and any water catcher.
6. Wipe the heating plate with a dry cloth so no grains burn on next time.

Rinse your rice before cooking, too. It washes off loose starch, which means less foam, less sticking and a cleaner lid. A [Japanese rice washing bowl with drainers](https://www.amazon.com/Inomata-Delicious-Japanese-Vegetable-Drainers/dp/B00IZ9IIPW/?tag=kitchenpot-20) makes that quick, and a [Marna standing rice paddle](https://www.amazon.com/Stand-Paddle-Shamoji-K-386W-Marna/dp/B003B66YRI/?tag=kitchenpot-20) is gentle on nonstick coatings. On pressure models, check the rubber packing in the lid every few months and replace it when it cracks or stops sealing.
